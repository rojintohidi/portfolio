Published Book Covers — Avidmand Publication

Designed four published book covers in Adobe Illustrator, and created and managed Instagram content and promotional materials for publication and marketing goals.

Role
Instagram Content Creator & Graphic Designer
For
Avidmand Publication
Date
2022–December 2024
Discipline
Graphic & Editorial

Overview

Over more than two years with Avidmand Publication, Rojin designed four published book covers in Adobe Illustrator alongside publication promotion. This gallery presents three confirmed cover spreads: Beyond the Limits (بی‌حد و مرز, 2024), Atomic Habits (عادت‌های اتمی, 2024), and George Orwell’s Animal Farm (مزرعه حیوانات, 2023).

Deliverables

  • Front, spine and back book cover design (Adobe Illustrator)
  • Persian editorial typography and print layout
  • Instagram content and promotional materials
