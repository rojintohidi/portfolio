Golestan Rose — Cut-Paper Animation

Self-initiated · 2026

An 8-second cut-paper loop animated on twos: the rose from the Golestan cover opens, holds, and lets its petals fall.

Role
2D animation & art direction
For
Self-initiated
Date
2026
Discipline
Animation

Overview

The rose is built from the same paper pieces as the Golestan cover in the Three Classics series. It is animated on twos, twelve drawings a second with each drawing held for two frames, and every piece shifts slightly between drawings, as hand-placed paper does under a stop-motion camera. The bud opens petal by petal with a small bounce, the heart closes again, and the outer petals detach one by one, turning over as they fall, until only the bud is left and the loop begins again. Falling pieces cast longer, softer shadows, so they read as lifted off the page.

Self-initiated. Drafted with AI assistance under my art direction.

Deliverables

  • 8 s seamless loop, 1080 × 1350, 24 fps
  • Animated on twos: 96 drawings
  • Cut-paper pieces from the Three Classics cover system
  • Poster still
